Add Monochrome Theme

Context:
Currently WealthSignal supports Light and Dark themes.

Goal:
Provide a third black and white minimalistic theme targeted at serious investors who prefer minimal visual distractions.

Requirements:
- No backend changes
- Persist theme selection
- Desktop and mobile support
- Existing charts remain readable
- Theme must work across all existing pages

Concerns:
- Accessibility
- Contrast ratios
- Chart readability

CEO Decision:
Option A approved — pure monochrome throughout. All decorative colour (amber, violet, emerald) replaced with white/gray. P&L positive/negative differentiated by white vs gray (not green/red). The +/- sign carries semantic meaning.
